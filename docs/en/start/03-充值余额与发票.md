# 03 - Balance, Top-ups, and Invoices

This page explains how to add funds, view your balance, understand deductions, review billing records, and request invoices.

## Adding funds

1. Sign in to the console and open the top-up page.
2. Select an amount and submit the request.
3. Scan the QR code to add customer support and send the top-up order number.
4. Follow the payment instructions, retain the payment receipt, and upload it.
5. Funds are credited after customer support approves the request.

## Viewing your balance

Your available balance is displayed on the top-up page. Add funds before the balance runs out to avoid service interruptions.

## Deduction rules

Charges follow the corresponding model provider's official billing rules.

## Bills and usage details

The dashboard shows overall model usage. The usage logs show the cost of each request.

## Requesting an invoice

You can request an invoice after an offline top-up has been credited. Select an eligible top-up record and submit the invoice information.

The minimum invoice amount is CNY 100. Amounts that have already been invoiced are non-refundable. Electronic invoices are normally issued within approximately seven business days and sent to your specified email address.

Invoice information cannot be changed after submission. For assistance, contact [contact@novaapis.com](mailto:contact@novaapis.com).
